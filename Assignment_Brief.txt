Scenario
Background
NetConnect Solutions, headquartered in Atlanta, is a leading telecommunications provider known for its broad range of services including high-speed internet, mobile networks, and cable television. Serving millions across the United States, NetConnect has a reputation for robust service and innovative solutions, catering to both residential and corporate sectors.

Prelude to the Crisis
In recent months, despite its strong market presence, NetConnect noticed an unsettling trend: an uptick in customer churn rates, particularly in densely populated urban areas. Traditional analyses, performed manually by teams of data analysts, hinted at various factors but failed to provide actionable insights or reveal deeper patterns that could be addressed proactively.

The Incident
On a sweltering Friday in July, just as the weekend was starting, a major outage struck. Originating from a cyber-attack that targeted their infrastructure, the outage incapacitated internet access for over 600,000 users for several hours. The timing was critical—impacting businesses at peak operation hours and disrupting communication services during a major sports event, which escalated customer dissatisfaction.

Immediate Aftermath
Customer service lines were instantly overwhelmed with calls, the website crashed under the load of complaints, and social media erupted with negative publicity. The incident not only strained operational capacities but also accelerated customer churn, particularly among high-value corporate clients and tech-savvy users, who expected higher reliability.

Strategic Shift
Recognizing the urgency to understand and mitigate these churn risks, NetConnect’s senior management decided to engage a Data Science team to overhaul their approach to data analysis. The goal was to move away from manual, reactive methods toward a sophisticated, automated analytics system.

Role of the Data Analyst
A skilled data analyst, Alex, was brought on board to lead this transformative project. Alex's first task was to develop a predictive model using machine learning to analyze existing big data for patterns in customer churn. Key attributes to be analyzed included:
    • Demographic Data: Age and geographical location.
    • Service Usage Patterns: Data usage, preferred services (internet, mobile, TV), and payment methods.
    • Incident Impact Reports: Specific data focusing on their direct impact on customer segments.
Objective
Alex's analysis aimed to identify not just the ‘who’ and ‘when’ of customer churn but also the ‘why’. 
By integrating these insights into an automated real-time analytics dashboard, NetConnect hoped to anticipate customer dissatisfaction and intervene before the decision to leave, thus transforming their data-driven strategy to be more proactive and less reactive.

Challenges Ahead
The challenges were immense:
    • Integrating diverse data sources into a coherent, accessible format for advanced analytics.
    • Ensuring data accuracy and consistency across multiple platforms.
    • Developing a user-friendly interface for the automated system that could be used by various departments within NetConnect.

Assignment Brief and Guidance
You are required to:

    1) Develop a series of training materials for the company (in audio/video/written form) - Provide an overview of key policy and market shifts in Telecom company.
    a) The training materials will need to explain the fundamental concepts of big data and its value in data driven decision making (DDDM) for end users and organisations, before moving on to predicting the impact of using new complex data sets for users and organisations.
    b) A discussion of the processes of DDDM when using complex data sets should also be included in the training materials.
    c) Discuss the advantages and challenges to an organisation of using complex data sets for decision making.

    2) Your team leader has asked you to decide on the best tools to create a visualization demo for prospective use in Telecom Analysis. You need to produce a business proposal assessing a range of known industry-leading tools and software, which is suitable for NetConnect Solutions.

    3) Develop a data visualisation demo for prospective use in their business strategy. This requires:
    a)  creating a dashboard using data from any recognised source of for example: https://www.kaggle.com/code/quickwater/intro-to-r-with-telco-customer-churn-data/notebook
    b) drawing a set of conclusions from the created data to demonstrate how NetConnect can leverage this in helping to drive its business decisions around customer retention.

    4) Produce an evaluation report of how the chosen data preparation and manipulation methods, the tools selected, and the data derived has aided well in the decision-making process for NetConnect.
